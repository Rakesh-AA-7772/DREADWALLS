DREADWALLS V2 — OPTIONAL DROP-IN ASSETS

Place these exact files beside index.html, style.css and game.js:

walls.png      - wall texture for the first-person renderer
door.png       - exit-door image displayed at the end of the maze
floor.jpg      - repeating floor texture rendered in perspective
moon.png       - full moon displayed in the sky when looking upward
into.mp3       - home/dashboard background music
game.mp3       - gameplay background music
monster.mp3    - one-shot sound when a shadow encounter begins
footsteps.mp3  - looping footsteps synchronized with player movement

All listed assets are optional. Missing image assets use the game's available fallback visuals; missing audio files are silently ignored by the browser.
